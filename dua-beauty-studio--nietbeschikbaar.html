DUA BEAUTY STUDIO ALMERE, THE NETHERLANDS
01 — OVERVIEW

A digital space for beauty and self-care.

DUA Beauty Studio is a beauty concept based in Almere, specialising in lash and brow treatments.

The goal was to create a refined online presence that reflects the studio's identity while making its treatments and services easy to explore.

The result is an elegant, editorial-inspired website built around simplicity, visual balance and a warm beauty aesthetic.

CLIENT DUA Beauty Studio
INDUSTRY Beauty & Wellness
LOCATION Almere, Netherlands

02 — THE APPROACH

Designed with intention.

A visual identity inspired by the beauty industry's balance between elegance and simplicity.

The design combines a warm neutral colour palette, expressive typography and generous whitespace. Every element was chosen to create a calm, sophisticated and consistent experience.
